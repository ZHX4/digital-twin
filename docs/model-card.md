# Model Card

## Model
**Name:** Digital Twin Research Simulator v3.0

**Purpose:** Explore synthetic future education architectures through longitudinal simulation.

## Intended use
- research prototyping
- systems thinking
- educational policy simulation
- experiment design
- model-governance analysis

## Not intended for
- real admissions
- real child profiling
- medical diagnosis
- genetic counseling
- hiring
- career or educational exclusion
- prediction of individual life outcomes

## Inputs
Synthetic random seed, environment parameters, learner state and synthetic genomic prior.

## Outputs
Longitudinal state estimates, pathway posteriors, curriculum actions, uncertainty, counterfactual effects, fairness and calibration diagnostics.

## Limitations
The mechanisms are hand-designed research models. They are not estimated from a representative population and are not validated causal or psychometric instruments.

## Governance
High uncertainty, early age, insufficient evidence and excessive genomic weighting trigger safety flags. Recommendations remain reversible.
