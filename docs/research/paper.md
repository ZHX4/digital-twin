# Digital Twin for Human Development

## A synthetic causal systems laboratory for competency-based education

### Abstract

This project makes a hypothetical education architecture executable as a seeded longitudinal simulator. It combines synthetic genomic priors, adaptive psychometrics, skill tracing, forgetting, active exploration, adaptive curriculum, pathway posteriors, governance, counterfactual interventions, equity stress tests, calibration diagnostics and a synthetic labor-market extension.

The contribution is methodological rather than empirical. It is not a model of real children and does not claim that genomic information can identify an ideal career.

## Research framing

Conventional education can be represented as age -> grade -> curriculum -> examination -> credential.

The proposed loop is observation -> state estimation -> exploration -> intervention -> new evidence.

## Limitations

All causal, psychometric, genetic and market mechanisms are synthetic and hand-designed. The platform has no external validity without empirical calibration and validation.

## Extensions

Future work should replace modules with empirically calibrated learner models, validated psychometrics, stronger causal models and governed external market data.
