# Research Protocol

## Questions

1. Does competency-oriented personalization reduce simulated pathway mismatch?
2. Does active exploration reduce premature specialization?
3. Does longitudinal evidence improve posterior calibration?
4. How sensitive are outcomes to mentorship, acceleration, environment and exploration?
5. Does a bounded synthetic genomic prior add information beyond longitudinal evidence?
6. Can personalization widen resource-access gaps?

## Discipline

Fix seeds for policy comparisons. Change one major intervention at a time. Report distributions, uncertainty and controls. Label all outputs synthetic.

## Outcomes

Primary: competency age, mismatch risk, pathway stability, wellbeing and posterior uncertainty.

Secondary: exploration coverage, agency proxy, opportunity cost, calibration error and synthetic equity gaps.

A simulation can falsify assumptions inside its model; it cannot establish that the same effect exists in real populations.
