# Future Interfaces

## AGI tutor

A future tutor could generate explanations, projects, simulations and assessments dynamically. It should retain competency checks, provenance, uncertainty, human override and learner agency.

## Neurotechnology

A future branch could study non-invasive neurophysiology as noisy evidence. The current system does not require a brain implant and does not treat neural signals as direct access to thoughts or destiny.

## Quantum computing

A future branch could test quantum optimization for curriculum planning. Quantum hardware is not required by the current system and does not substitute for human-development evidence.
